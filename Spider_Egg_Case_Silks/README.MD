# Fundamental Studies on Spider Egg Case Silk Biomaterials and Their Mimics
### Principal Investigators: Dr. Jeffery L. Yarger (jyarger@gmail.com)

### National Science Foundation (NSF) Award 2105312 (2021-2026). 

Silks produced by nature are among the most remarkable biopolymers, possessing an exceptional combination of lightweight density, high mechanical strength, and biocompatibility. While most prior research has focused on silkworms or spider dragline silk, spiders also produce specialized egg case silks designed to protect their developing offspring against extreme mechanical shocks, predators, and environmental stress. Supported by the National Science Foundation, researchers at Arizona State University conducted a comprehensive investigation into the molecular structure, physical properties, and potential future applications of spider egg case silks and numerous related silk biopolymers.
